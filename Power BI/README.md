# Business Intelligence Dashboard 

## Project Overview
Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to turn raw sales data into useful business insights.

Analyzed revenue, sales performance, product categories, states, and monthly trends.

Created DAX measures for Total Revenue, Previous Month Revenue, and MoM Growth %.

Added a dynamic Performance Insight to show whether revenue was growing, declining, or remaining unchanged.

Used slicers, tooltips, and interactive visuals to make the report easier to explore.

Applied Power BI storytelling techniques to present important findings clearly.

The project helps management monitor business performance, identify trends, and understand areas that may require attention.

The project covers data preparation, data modelling, DAX calculation, visualization, and business insights using microsoft Power BI.


## Business problem
The dashboard was design to answer questions such as:

1. What happened to revenue compared with the previous month?
2. Which category is performing best?
3. State that's performing best?
4. What the overall business story was?
5. what management should investigate and find a solution to?
6. how many unique customers made purchase?
7. How revenue is changing overtime?
8. How many orders were made?
9. Which area is performing above or below expectations?
10. What were the driving business driving performances?
11. What was the average order value?
